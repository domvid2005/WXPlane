WXPlane - third-party notices
=============================

WXPlane includes the following open-source software. Their licences require these notices to accompany the plugin.

  * OpenJPEG 2.5.3 (JPEG 2000 decoding of NOAA GTGN turbulence data) - 2-clause BSD licence
  * libaec 1.1.3 (CCSDS decoding of ECMWF Open Data) - 2-clause BSD licence
  * libcurl (downloads) - curl licence (MIT/X derivative). The Windows build links it statically, with zlib (zlib licence).

Data used by WXPlane (not included in the plugin, downloaded while flying):
  * NOAA / National Weather Service: Aviation Weather Center (METAR, pilot reports), MRMS radar (reflectivity, echo tops,
    precipitation type), GTGN turbulence nowcast (GTG developed by NCAR for the FAA). Public domain.
  * Open-Meteo.com, CC BY 4.0 (https://open-meteo.com): NOAA NCEP GFS model data - winds and temperatures aloft, clouds aloft
    and the weather over open water.
  * ECMWF Open Data, (c) ECMWF, CC BY 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/): turbulence outside the US.
  * D-ATIS by clowd.io (ATIS text).
